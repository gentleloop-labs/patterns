Private OCD journaling, factual Calm Insights, and guided ERP practice. No account, no ads, and one optional lifetime Pro unlock.
description 618
Patterns is a private OCD journal and self-help practice companion. Record intrusive thoughts and compulsions, write journal entries, delay an urge, and practise exposure and response prevention (ERP). Calm Insights can replace calculated scores and streaks with factual activity counts. Personal content stays on your device unless you choose to export an unencrypted JSON backup or PDF. Optional anonymous usage analytics are off by default and never include OCD content, summaries, locale, or language choice. Patterns does not diagnose or treat OCD and is not a replacement for qualified care or emergency support.
whatsNew 273
Patterns 1.10 introduces optional Calm Insights, quieter endings after journal and practice sessions, and optional English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, Japanese, Spanish, and French interfaces. Your entries stay as written and your language choice remains on this device.

description 670
Patterns é um diário privado para transtorno obsessivo-compulsivo (TOC) e uma ferramenta de autoajuda. Registre pensamentos intrusivos e compulsões, escreva no diário, adie um impulso e pratique exposição e prevenção de resposta (EPR). Patterns não diagnostica nem trata TOC e não substitui cuidado qualificado nem suporte de emergência.

Patternsは、OCDの記録と練習を支えるプライベートなセルフヘルプツールです。侵入思考や強迫行為を記録し、日記を書き、衝動を遅らせ、曝露反応妨害（ERP）を練習できます。穏やかなインサイトでは、計算されたスコアや連続記録の代わりに事実に基づく活動回数を表示できます。個人の内容は、暗号化されていないJSONまたはPDFを自分で書き出さない限り端末内に残ります。匿名の利用状況分析は初期状態でオフで、OCDの内容、概要、地域設定、言語選択は含みません。PatternsはOCDの診断や治療を行わず、専門家によるケアや緊急支援の代わりではありません。

Les analyses anonymes sont facultatives, désactivées par défaut et n’incluent jamais contenu TOC, résumés, région ou langue. Patterns ne diagnostique ni ne traite le TOC et ne remplace pas un professionnel ni une aide d’urgence.
