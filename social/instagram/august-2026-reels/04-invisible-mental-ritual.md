## Purpose

Help viewers recognize mental review as a compulsion by showing an outwardly still person and an active internal OCD voice. Connect the insight to short, non-judgmental tracking in Patterns.

- Date: Aug 30
- Duration: 24 seconds
- Background: static desk with a faint rewind symbol

## Final dialogue

**Maya:** I'm not doing any compulsions.

**Loop:** Perfect. Let's replay that conversation.

**Maya:** Again?

**Loop:** Until every detail feels certain.

**Maya:** Wait. Replaying is the compulsion.

**Loop:** But we're just thinking.

**Maya:** Thinking can become a ritual when it is chasing certainty.

**Maya:** I can notice the loop without finishing it.
