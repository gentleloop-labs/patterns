## Recommended campaign set

Use **Intro → Resonance → Near Light → snowfall → A Walk** for a cohesive campaign in which each reel retains its own emotional identity.

## Mixing

- Keep music around **8–12% beneath dialogue**.
- Raise it slightly during the final product reveal.
- Add licensed music inside Instagram rather than baking it into the video master.

## Licensing and paid use

Instagram's licensed catalog varies by region and account type. Some business accounts and commercial posts may not have access to these songs. Confirm availability from the posting account before publishing.

For boosted reels or ads, select comparable instrumental tracks from Meta Sound Collection. Useful mood searches are:

- `minimal tension`
- `digital ambient`
- `gradual cinematic`
- `introspective ambient`
- `warm electronic`
