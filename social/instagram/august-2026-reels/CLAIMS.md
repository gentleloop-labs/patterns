# Claims gate

Every factual product or clinical statement used in the five reel scripts is listed here before video production.

## Verified product claims

- Patterns includes a Compulsion Delay tool that lets someone name an urge, rate it, choose a delay, and reflect afterward.
- Patterns includes OCD event tracking with obsession or compulsion context, response notes, and a 0-10 distress rating.
- Patterns does not provide an AI chatbot that answers an obsession.
- Patterns Pro includes an Exposure Hierarchy tool.
- Patterns Pro includes an Urge Surfing tool with timing and reflection.
- Patterns supports personal reflection and ERP practice but does not diagnose OCD or replace qualified care.

Product evidence: repository `README.md`, `website/src/lib/data/faq.ts`, app source under `lib/mobile/screens/`, and approved app-store assets.

## Verified clinical framing

- Compulsions may be visible actions or mental acts.
- Reassurance seeking and repeated checking can function as compulsions.
- ERP combines exposure with response prevention.
- ERP is planned, gradual, and ideally guided by a clinician trained in OCD.
- Urge surfing observes an urge without requiring immediate action or forcing the urge to disappear.

Clinical references: International OCD Foundation and NIMH links in `../august-2026-posts/SOURCES.md`.

## Do not add without evidence

- Recovery rates, symptom-reduction percentages, user counts, ratings, reviews, or testimonials
- Claims that Patterns treats or cures OCD
- Claims that a tool will make anxiety or an urge disappear
- Claims that self-guided ERP is appropriate for every person or situation
