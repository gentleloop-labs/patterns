# Reel 02: Searching for certainty in 12 browser tabs

## Purpose

Show how digital reassurance seeking can add more information without satisfying the demand for certainty. Position Patterns as a place to record the response pattern, not answer the obsession.

## Specs

- Date: Aug 28
- Duration: 25 seconds
- Background: static desk, laptop, and stack of twelve browser tabs
- Product ending: code-native OCD event tracker poster
- Hook text: `12 TABS. SAME QUESTION.`

## Fixed composition

- Maya remains waist-up beside the laptop in the lower-left.
- Loop remains inside the thought cloud in the upper-right.
- Twelve browser-tab cards remain fixed in the background.
- The tabs do not multiply, scroll, or move.

## Final dialogue

**Maya:** One more search and I'll know for sure.

**Loop:** Great. Open this result too.

**Maya:** It says the same thing.

**Loop:** But what if the next one doesn't?

**Maya:** I've opened twelve tabs and the question is still here.

**Loop:** Thirteen could be the lucky one.

**Maya:** Or I can stop chasing an answer.

**Maya:** Patterns records the loop. It does not answer the obsession.
