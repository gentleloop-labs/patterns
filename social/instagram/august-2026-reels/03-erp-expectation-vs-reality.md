# Reel 03: What people think ERP is vs. what it actually is

## Purpose

Correct the idea that ERP is forced flooding or a dramatic confrontation. Explain planned exposure and response prevention, then show the real Patterns exposure hierarchy.

## Specs

- Date: Aug 29
- Duration: 24 seconds
- Background: static four-rung exposure ladder diagram
- Product ending: code-native Exposure Hierarchy poster for Patterns Pro
- Hook text: `ERP IS NOT A STUNT`

## Fixed composition

- Maya remains waist-up in the lower-left.
- Loop remains inside the thought cloud in the upper-right.
- A simple exposure ladder remains fixed between or behind them.
- Nothing climbs the ladder and no cliff is shown.

## Final dialogue

**Loop:** ERP means facing your biggest fear at once.

**Maya:** No. Good ERP is planned and gradual.

**Loop:** So the goal is to feel calm?

**Maya:** The goal is to meet uncertainty without the usual compulsion.

**Loop:** That sounds harder than jumping.

**Maya:** It is practice. Not a stunt.

**Maya:** Build the ladder with an OCD-trained clinician. Organize the steps in Patterns.
