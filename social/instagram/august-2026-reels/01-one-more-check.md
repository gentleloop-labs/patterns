# Reel 01: Just check one more time

## Purpose

Make the checking loop recognizable, then show a small response change. Introduce the Patterns Compulsion Delay tool without implying that the urge disappears.

## Specs

- Date: Aug 27
- Duration: 22 seconds
- Background: static front door and lock
- Product ending: code-native Compulsion Delay poster
- Hook text: `ONE MORE CHECK`

## Fixed composition

- Maya remains waist-up in the lower-left.
- Loop remains inside the thought cloud in the upper-right.
- The door and lock remain fixed behind them.
- Only expressions, bubble text, captions, and speaker emphasis change.

## Final dialogue

**Loop:** Just check the lock one more time.

**Maya:** I already checked it.

**Loop:** Right. So one more check will make you certain.

**Maya:** That's what you said last time.

**Loop:** This time is different.

**Maya:** Maybe. I'm leaving without checking again.

**Loop:** But what if...

**Maya:** The question can stay open. Patterns can help me pause before I respond.
