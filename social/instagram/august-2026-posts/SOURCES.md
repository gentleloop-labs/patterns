# Sources

## OCD and ERP

- International OCD Foundation, "About OCD": https://iocdf.org/about-ocd/
- International OCD Foundation, "Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)": https://iocdf.org/about-ocd/ocd-treatment-guide/erp/
- International OCD Foundation, "Digital Reassurance Seeking in OCD": https://iocdf.org/blog/2026/07/21/digital-reassurance-seeking-in-ocd/
- National Institute of Mental Health, "Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder": https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/obsessive-compulsive-disorder-when-unwanted-thoughts-or-repetitive-behaviors-take-over

These sources support the descriptions of the obsession-compulsion cycle, visible and mental compulsions, reassurance seeking, exposure hierarchies, response prevention, and clinician-guided ERP.

## Patterns

- Product site: https://patternsocd.com/
- Repository overview: `../../../README.md`
- Product FAQ: `../../../website/src/lib/data/faq.ts`

Patterns is described as a private, local-first OCD journaling, tracking, and ERP support app. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure OCD and is not a replacement for qualified professional care.
