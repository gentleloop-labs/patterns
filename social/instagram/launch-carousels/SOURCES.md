## Post 5 — Howie Mandel

### Interviews and reporting

- Cathy Cassata, [Comedian Howie Mandel’s OCD Diagnosis Was a “Huge Weight” Off His Shoulders](https://www.healthline.com/health-news/how-howie-mandel-was-finally-diagnosed-after-decades-of-living-with-ocd), Healthline, updated 13 October 2023. ## Editorial note

These posts summarize public accounts; they do not diagnose either subject or generalize one person’s symptoms to everyone with OCD. The closing cards include a non-diagnostic care boundary consistent with the existing Patterns launch carousels.
