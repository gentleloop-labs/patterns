# Generated motif prompts

The three text-free cover motifs were created with the built-in image-generation tool. All typography, UI components, and final composition were then rendered deterministically with the app's bundled fonts and brand palette.

## 1. Pattern rings

Create a premium, restrained abstract visual that suggests noticing recurring mental patterns: concentric incomplete rings and a small centered dot, subtly irregular, calm rather than hypnotic. Use a deep charcoal-to-black field, a refined soft 3D paper-and-glass treatment, and very soft warm amber rim light. Keep the motif centered with generous negative space. Palette: `#141414`, `#1E1E1E`, `#F4C95D`, and tiny touches of `#F4F0E8`. No text, logos, people, medical imagery, watermark, bright neon, psychedelic spirals, or clutter. Vertical 4:5.

## 2. Daily path

Create a premium, restrained abstract visual that suggests a gentle sequence: six rounded stepping forms or softly illuminated nodes following a calm curved path from observation to reflection. Use a deep charcoal-to-black field, soft 3D paper-and-glass materials, and warm amber light progressing subtly along the path. Palette: `#141414`, `#1E1E1E`, `#F4C95D`, `#7BBF91`, and `#F4F0E8`. Exactly six simple forms. No arrows, numbers, text, logos, people, medical imagery, watermark, neon, or clutter. Vertical 4:5.

## 3. Private space

Create a premium, restrained abstract visual that suggests a private, protected space for reflection: a small warm glowing sphere held inside two offset curved charcoal shells, open enough to feel breathable rather than locked away. Use matte charcoal shells, a frosted-glass sphere, subtle paper grain, and warm amber light on a deep charcoal-to-black field. No padlock, shield, keyhole, text, logos, people, medical imagery, watermark, cybersecurity clichés, cage imagery, neon, or clutter. Vertical 4:5.
