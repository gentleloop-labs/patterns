# Illustration prompts

Built-in image generation was used in `scientific-educational` mode. The supplied Instagram screenshot was used only as a loose style reference. Each image was generated as a text-free illustration, then the background was extracted to genuine transparency before layout.

## Shared direction

Polished flat vector-like editorial illustration for a 1080 × 1350 Instagram mental-health infographic. Soft organic shapes, subtle paper texture, warm and emotionally intelligent, adult rather than childish, inclusive, clean silhouette and restrained details. Patterns palette: charcoal, warm cream, golden yellow, muted sage, muted coral and dusty blue. Centered character vignette with generous edges. No words, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI, photorealism, medical symbols or diagnostic claims.

## 01 · Reassurance loop

A gender-neutral adult looks at a blank-screen phone with a worried expression while three empty rounded speech bubbles orbit in a circular loop and return to the phone, suggesting repeated checking and brief relief.

## 02 · Mental compulsions

A gender-neutral adult touches their temple while simple looping ribbons and abstract thought cards circle behind and partly within the head silhouette, suggesting reviewing, analyzing, checking feelings and neutralizing.

## 03 · ERP has two parts

A gender-neutral adult takes one manageable step toward a small abstract trigger shape while leaving an empty ritual/checking icon behind; a gentle two-step pathway communicates exposure plus response prevention.

## 04 · Urgent doubt

A gender-neutral adult stands beside a large abstract alarm bell, notices it and calmly holds up an open palm while a small hourglass rests nearby; their body turns back toward an ordinary activity such as a book or plant.

## 05 · Recovery progress

A gender-neutral adult walks forward carrying a small cloud-shaped feeling beside them rather than fighting it; stepping stones sit behind and a warm plant/star ahead represents values. The scene communicates movement and self-compassion, not victory or cure.

## Background extraction edit

Remove the visible checkerboard and make it genuinely transparent. Change only the background; preserve the illustration, character, colors, texture, shapes, proportions and edges. No new objects, words, logos, watermark, crop or redesign.
