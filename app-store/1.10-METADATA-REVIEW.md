## Required review gate

Before any non-English metadata is moved into canonical `metadata/` or uploaded, a native reviewer must approve OCD, ERP, Y-BOCS, privacy, crisis, and safety terminology. Mexico and Spain, and France and Canada, need separate keyword/subtitle review even though they share in-app translations.

The matching 1.10 multilingual binary and localized screenshots must be ready before upload. After approval, upload one locale at a time and verify every field in App Store Connect.
