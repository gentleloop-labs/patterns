Today, I let the thought be there.

The urge to check showed up again before I left. I noticed it, named it, and walked away without going back.

It felt uncomfortable—not dangerous. I did not need to solve the feeling or make it disappear.

The uncertainty stayed with me for a while. Then, without another check, it became quieter on its own.

I kept moving through the evening instead of returning to the door. The doubt came back twice, and I let it pass twice.

I do not need certainty to choose what matters next.

Showing up for the practice was enough today.
