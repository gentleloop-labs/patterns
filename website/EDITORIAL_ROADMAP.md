# Patterns OCD Library: 90-Day Editorial Roadmap

This queue supports one carefully sourced article each week. Do not publish a draft until its sources, internal links, safety language, and confidential factual-review gate are complete.

## Publishing workflow

1. Choose the next query and confirm that an existing page does not already answer the same intent.
2. Write the direct answer first, then outline the reader's likely follow-up questions.
3. Use public health guidance, professional OCD organizations, treatment guidelines, and peer-reviewed research for clinical claims.
4. Add first-person material only when Aftaab has genuine, relevant experience to contribute.
5. Link to one cornerstone guide and at least two closely related articles using descriptive anchor text.
6. Run the private factual-review gate. Keep `draft: true` until feedback is resolved.
7. Run `npm test`, `npm run check`, and `npm run build` from `website/`.
8. Change `draft` to `false`, publish, request indexing if appropriate, and record the publication in the measurement log.

## Article acceptance checklist

- The title, introduction, and meta description answer one distinct search intent.
- The article does not diagnose the reader, prescribe an individual plan, or guarantee recovery.
- Clinical claims have inspectable sources and statistics have direct citations.
- Examples clarify function without supplying unnecessary graphic detail or reassurance.
- The category and `contentType` are accurate, and any lived experience is genuine.
- The FAQ answers stand alone without overstating certainty.
- Publication and meaningful-update dates are accurate.
- The confidential reviewer is not named or presented as endorsing the article.
- The app invitation follows the informational answer and accurately describes Patterns as a journal and ERP companion.

## Measurement rhythm

- Before the first publication, record indexed pages, non-brand clicks, non-brand impressions, and app CTA clicks as the baseline.
- At weeks 4, 8, and 12, record the same metrics plus the queries and landing pages gaining impressions.
- Investigate pages that compete for the same query before drafting adjacent content. Consolidate or retarget rather than publishing a near-duplicate.
- Measure early progress through indexation and relevant query coverage. Avoid promising a traffic number before enough Search Console data exists.
